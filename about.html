Indigo Alphabets® is an independent type foundry led by Iñigo Uriarte and based in Berlin.

The project aspires to fill gaps in the typographic panorama, push the field forward and platform the explorative pulse of the designer.

Especialized in futuristic, blackletter and bitmap faces, Indigo Alphabets is commited to the highest standard of quality in its work.
